Lunch & Nutrition

Lunch is served Monday – Friday, 12:00 – 1:00 PM.

  • Free for people aged 60 and older. Persons 60+ are urged to give a contribution toward the meal cost.
  • Anyone under 60 can buy a meal for $5.00.
  • Lunches are first-come, first-served. Reservations are not required.

Registration is required

Before your first lunch, either:

  1. Print and fill out the Lunch Registration Form, and bring it with you on the first day you'd like a meal, or
  2. Stop by the center and fill out the paperwork in person on your first day.
